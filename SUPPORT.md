# Support

## How to Get Help

**AURA_Marketplace** is maintained by the SWProductivity team at Vitesco Technologies.

- **Bug reports and feature requests**: Open an issue in the [AURA_Marketplace repository](https://github.vitesco.io/SWProductivity/AURA_Marketplace/issues). Search existing issues before opening a new one to avoid duplicates.
- **Contribution questions**: Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) first. If your question is not covered, open a discussion or issue.
- **General questions**: Reach out to [@SWProductivity/aura-maintainers](https://github.vitesco.io/orgs/SWProductivity/teams/aura-maintainers) on GitHub Enterprise.

## Support Policy

Support is provided on a best-effort basis by the AURA maintainers team. Response times may vary depending on team capacity.
